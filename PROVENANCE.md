## English

### Known provenance
The character is Inori Yuzuriha from Guilty Crown. The [official character page](https://guilty-crown.jp/character/) credits **© GUILTY CROWN COMMITTEE**.

Project preparation and user-directed creation are credited to **tyxkk2**. The assets are AI-assisted unofficial fan art. Outfit labels identify variants rather than asserting exact official costume reproduction.

The project reuses five completed sprite PNGs, five individual preview GIFs, and one chibi comparison GIF. The three chibi PNGs came from an existing validated bundle; the two standard-proportion sheets came from existing individual PNGs. Their bytes are unchanged. This update concerns documentation, notices, package contents, and integrity records.

Reference images and private production records are not included in the candidate payload.

### Open provenance and rights questions
A complete source/author/terms record for the initial reference images is not yet available for publication review. The official character page establishes character attribution; it does not establish the source or permission for every input reference image. No public-redistribution permission for underlying character or reference-image rights is asserted.

The [Production I.G policy](https://www.production-ig.co.jp/sitepolicy) is recorded as a material restriction for review, not a license. No title-specific fan-content permission or authorization to publish these assets has been confirmed.
